How to use 3S Admin for 3S Global Greens

3S Admin lets the 3S Global Greens team update 3sglobalgreens.com without a developer: contact details, leadership, certificates, figures, products, the process, ESG, plant photos and careers - and follow up the buy and sell enquiries the website receives. This guide walks through every screen with pictures.

How a change reaches the website

Every update follows the same four steps.

1. Sign in

Password plus the 6-digit code from your phone.

2. Edit and Save

Open a section, change it, press Save.

3. Publish

Dashboard → Publish to website.

4. Live

The website is rebuilt with your changes. Refresh 3sglobalgreens.com after about 5 minutes.

Getting started

Signing in

Open 3sgroupadmin.com in any browser - computer or phone. Use the admin login shared with you.

The 6-digit code

Every sign-in asks for the code from the authenticator app on your phone (Google or Microsoft Authenticator). The first time, the admin shows a QR code to scan with the app.

You are signed out automatically

Closing the browser signs you out, and so do 2 hours without any activity. Just sign in again - saved work is safe.

Getting started

Choosing the website

After signing in you see every 3S Group website your login can edit.

Getting started

The dashboard and the Publish button

The dashboard is the home of the website: everything you can edit, new enquiries, and recent changes.

Save, then Publish

Save keeps your change in the admin. Publish to website rebuilds 3sglobalgreens.com with all saved changes. Make all your changes first and publish once at the end - each Publish takes about 5 minutes.

If Publish says the website could not be rebuilt

Your changes are still saved. Try Publish again after a few minutes; if it keeps failing, contact the 3S Group team.

Everyday tasks

Company profile

The text that describes the company across the site.

Lists

Boxes that say "One per line" hold a list: each line becomes one point on the website. Press Enter to start the next point.

Everyday tasks

Contact details

Phone numbers and emails used in the footer, menu, WhatsApp button, Contact and Careers pages, and the legal pages.

Check before saving

A wrong number or email here changes it everywhere on the website, including the WhatsApp button.

Everyday tasks

Leadership

The leadership cards on the About page. Add a card for a new director, or edit an existing one.

Everyday tasks

Certifications

Each ISO certificate on the Certifications page, with its number, dates, issuer and scope. Add a certificate when a new one is issued; update the dates when one is renewed.

Everyday tasks

Figures

Processing capacity, recovery, water recirculated, energy, people, safe days, and the recovery figures on the home page. A figure only appears on the website once it has a number - so nothing unverified is ever shown.

Everyday tasks

Products, services and industries

Each product, service and industry has its own photo, text and lists.

  1. Open the section in the menu.
  2. Click an item to change it, or Add for a new one.
  3. Use the arrows on the list to change the order on the website. Numbered items (process stages, selling steps) renumber themselves.
  4. Click Save, then Publish to website on the dashboard.

Everyday tasks

Plant gallery

The plant photos. Each photo is shown in one place: the home page slider, the pollution control gallery or the storage gallery on the About page.

Enquiries and reports

Feedback & Leads

Every enquiry sent from the enquiry form on the Contact page - customers buying refined lead and suppliers selling lead-bearing material. Enquiries are also emailed to the team as before.

Career applications

Applications with a resume are emailed to HR as before; they do not appear under Leads.

Enquiries and reports

Activity log and Analytics

The Activity log records every change made in the admin - what was changed, in which section, and when. Analytics shows website visitor numbers once the 3S Group team connects Google Analytics for the site.

Help

Hide or delete?

Most items can be hidden or deleted. When in doubt, hide.

Hide (eye icon)
  • Disappears from the website after Publish.
  • Stays in the admin, greyed out.
  • Bring it back any time with one click.
Delete permanently (bin icon)
  • Removed from the admin and the website.
  • Cannot be undone.
  • Use for mistakes and old items nobody needs.

Help

Common questions

I published but the website still shows the old version.

Publishing rebuilds the website, which takes about 5 minutes. Wait, then refresh the page. If it still shows the old version after 10 minutes, press Publish again.

I forgot the password.

On the sign-in screen click Forgot password?. A code is emailed to the admin inbox. Enter it and choose a new password.

The phone with the authenticator app was lost or changed.

Contact the 3S Group web team. They can reset the authenticator so you can scan a new QR code.

A photo will not upload.

Photos must be JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF and under 10 MB. Large phone photos can be sent to yourself on WhatsApp first, which makes them smaller.

A figure does not show on the website.

A figure only shows once it has a number. Open Impact Figures or Recovery Figures, type the number, Save and Publish.

I deleted something by mistake.

If you chose Just hide it, open the item and tick Published again. A permanent delete cannot be undone - add the item again.
